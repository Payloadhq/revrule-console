RECORDING AGREEMENT

This Recording Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2026,
by and between:

ARTIST: Maya Chen ("Artist")
LABEL: Indie Records LLC ("Label")

1. RECORDING COMMITMENT
Artist agrees to deliver one (1) album consisting of ten (10) tracks.

2. ARTIST ROYALTY
Artist shall receive a royalty of 18% of net receipts from the sale and
streaming of the Album. Net receipts means gross revenue less distributor
fees, platform fees, and taxes.

3. PRODUCER POINTS
Producer Alex Rivera shall receive 3 points (3%) of net receipts from the
Album, payable from Artist's royalty share.

4. MANAGER COMMISSION
Manager Jordan Blake shall receive 15% of Artist's gross earnings from the
Album, calculated on Artist's royalty after producer points.

5. ADVANCE AND RECOUPMENT
Label shall pay Artist a recording advance of $25,000. The advance shall be
recouped from Artist's royalties at 100% of Artist's share until fully
recouped. After recoupment, Artist's full royalty rate applies.

6. SYNC LICENSING
For sync licensing revenue, Artist shall receive 50% of net sync revenue,
Producer shall receive 5% of net sync revenue.

7. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be three (3) years from the date of execution,
with an option for Label to extend for two (2) additional years.

8. TERRITORY
The territory for this Agreement shall be worldwide.

9. ACCOUNTING
Label shall provide royalty statements semi-annually, within 90 days of each
June 30 and December 31.
